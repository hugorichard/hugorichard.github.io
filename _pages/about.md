I am a Post doc at [ENSAE-CREST](https://crest.science/) under the supervision of [Vianney Perchet](https://www.ensae.fr/faculty/vianney-perchet/)

My research interests touch several areas of machine learning: I study theoretical properties of ML boosted algorithms, I build fast algorithms for unsupervised analysis of multiview datasets, I model and analyze neuroimaging data. 


I did my PhD at Inria Saclay in the [INRIA Parietal Team](https://team.inria.fr/parietal/) under the supervision of [Bertrand Thirion](https://pages.saclay.inria.fr/bertrand.thirion/).
