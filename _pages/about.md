I am a Research Scientist at [Criteo AI Labs](https://ailab.criteo.com/) and an industrial member of the [Fairplay team](https://team.inria.fr/fairplay/team-members/)  a joint team between Criteo, ENSAE and INRIA.

My current research focus is on reinforcement learning, bandits and differential privacy with application to advertising.

I did my PhD at Inria Saclay in the [INRIA Parietal Team](https://team.inria.fr/parietal/) under the supervision of [Bertrand Thirion](https://pages.saclay.inria.fr/bertrand.thirion/) on unsupervised learning algorithms for brain imaging.

I did my Post doc on bandits and ML-informed algorthms at [ENSAE-CREST](https://crest.science/) under the supervision of [Vianney Perchet](https://www.ensae.fr/faculty/vianney-perchet/)
