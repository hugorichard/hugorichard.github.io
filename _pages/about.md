I am a Research Scientist at [Criteo AI Labs](https://www.criteo.com/).

My research interests touch several areas of machine learning: 
I am interested in bandits and online algorithms, unsupervised analysis of multiview dataset and neuroimaging data.

I did my PhD at Inria Saclay in the [INRIA Parietal Team](https://team.inria.fr/parietal/) under the supervision of [Bertrand Thirion](https://pages.saclay.inria.fr/bertrand.thirion/).
I did my Post doc at [ENSAE-CREST](https://crest.science/) under the supervision of [Vianney Perchet](https://www.ensae.fr/faculty/vianney-perchet/)
